What Tarifly does

Save it your way

You save food videos and never cook them, because the recipe is buried in a caption, spoken in a voice-over, or flashed on screen. Share it to Tarifly from where you found it.

TikTok links

Share the post to Tarifly; it reads the caption and cover image.

Instagram links

Share the post to Tarifly; it reads the public post’s caption and cover image. If Instagram doesn’t show them, add a screenshot or paste the caption.

Screenshots

Up to six at a time, read in the order you choose.

Videos saved to Photos

Speech and on-screen text are read on your iPhone. Tarifly doesn't download videos from links.

Pasted text

Paste a recipe, or write one yourself.

Review

Check it before you save

Tarifly reads what you give it on your iPhone, lays out the ingredients and steps, and shows you everything before you save. When an amount isn't in the source, it says so instead of guessing.

FAQ

Good to know

Where can I save recipes from?

TikTok and Instagram links (Tarifly reads the post’s caption and cover image), screenshots — up to six at a time — videos saved to Photos, pasted text, or a recipe you write yourself. Tarifly doesn't download videos from links.

Does Tarifly read Instagram links?

Yes. Tarifly opens the post’s public page on your iPhone, without signing in, and reads its caption and cover image. Instagram sometimes doesn’t show the caption, or limits access for a few minutes after frequent requests; then take a screenshot of the recipe or paste the caption.

What if an amount or the whole recipe is missing?

When an amount isn't in the source, Tarifly says so instead of guessing. When a post has no recipe in it, Tarifly asks for a screenshot or the video — it never makes one up. A recipe with missing steps or ingredients can be completed later from a screenshot, video or text.

Is Tarifly free?

Yes. Tarifly Free includes up to 5 imports per rolling 7-day window (an import counts when you save it; failed or cancelled imports and recipes you write yourself don't count), unlimited saved recipes, a meal plan for the next 3 days, calorie and macro estimates, cook mode, and a shopping list — at no cost, with no account.

Tarifly Plus adds unlimited supported recipe imports, the full 7-day meal plan with Fill week automatically and Add week to shopping, serving scale and aisle-grouped shopping lists. Localized prices are shown in the App Store before purchase.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

Some import methods require a compatible Apple Intelligence device: recipes written as paragraphs are read with on-device Apple Intelligence. On other iPhones, recipes written as lists still import. Cloud processing is not currently available.

Where are my recipes kept?

On your iPhone. Your recipes, meal plan, shopping list and preferences are not sent to us or synced to any Tarifly server, and there is no account. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, that backup includes them.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

At Tarifly, your privacy is a product feature—not a marketing line. Your recipes belong to you.

1. Data collection & processing

  • No account required. Tarifly does not ask you to create an account or provide a name, phone number, or social login to use the app.
  • On your iPhone. Recipes are read on your iPhone: on-device Apple Intelligence where available, and on-device text recognition for screenshots and video frames. Your recipes, meal plan, shopping list and preferences are stored on your iPhone (SwiftData) and are not sent to us or synced to any Tarifly server. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, that backup includes them; backups are handled by Apple under your Apple Account.
  • TikTok links. When you share a TikTok link, Tarifly asks TikTok for that post’s caption and cover image. As with any web request, TikTok receives the link and your network address. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • Instagram links. When you share an Instagram link, Tarifly opens that post’s public page on your iPhone — without signing in and without keeping cookies — to read its caption, author name and cover image. As with any web visit, Instagram receives the link and your network address. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • No cloud processing. In this version Tarifly does not send recipe content, captions, screenshots, video frames or transcripts to any server for processing. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first and the app will say so before anything is sent.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal data.
  • Share Extension. When you share a link, screenshot or video into Tarifly, it is processed on your iPhone only to extract a recipe for you. Shared media is deleted once the import is saved or cancelled; a failed import keeps it so you can retry, until you discard it. One image may be kept on your iPhone as the recipe’s cover: the photo part of a screenshot, one still frame from a video, or the post’s cover image for a link.

2. Subscriptions & payments

Tarifly Plus subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes payment. We do not receive or store your full card number.

3. Analytics

Tarifly keeps a small set of counters on your device so we can tell whether the app is actually useful — for example how many recipes were imported, whether a cook session was finished, and whether a shopping list was ticked off. These counters are stored in a separate on-device database, are kept at day-level resolution, and are deleted after 90 days.

They never leave your iPhone. There is no analytics SDK, no third-party tracker, and no upload: Tarifly has no server that receives them and no account to attach them to. No advertising identifier, device identifier, or user identifier is generated or stored, and no recipe title, link, creator name, or caption is ever recorded — only fixed event labels. Deleting the app deletes them.

We use no crash-reporting SDK. Aggregate crash and download statistics provided by Apple in App Store Connect are anonymised by Apple before we see them; we never receive per-user data.
