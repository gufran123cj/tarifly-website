Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

At Tarifly, your privacy is a product feature—not a marketing line. Your recipes belong to you.

1. Data collection & processing

  • No account required. Tarifly does not ask you to create an account or provide a name, phone number, or social login to use the app.
  • On-device by default. Recipe extraction uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone when available. Saved recipes live in on-device storage (SwiftData)—not in a Tarifly user cloud account.
  • Optional cloud fallback (Plus only). On devices without Apple Intelligence, Tarifly Plus may send extraction requests through a secure proxy so the feature still works. Free-tier users without on-device AI are not silently billed; Plus is required for that path. We do not use that pipeline to sell ads or build advertising profiles.
  • No tracking for ads. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.
  • Share Extension. When you share a link or video into Tarifly, that content is processed only to extract a recipe for you.

2. Subscriptions & payments

Tarifly Plus subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes payment. We do not receive or store your full card number.

3. Analytics & crash data

If we enable diagnostic tooling in the future, it will be disclosed here and in App Store privacy labels. Prefer on-device diagnostics over third-party trackers.

4. Children’s privacy

Tarifly is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected in the App Store listing where required.
