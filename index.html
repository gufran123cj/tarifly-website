What Tarifly does

You save food videos and never cook them, because the recipe is buried in a caption, spoken in a voice-over, or flashed on screen. Tarifly reads what you give it on your iPhone, lays out the ingredients and steps, and shows you everything before you save. When an amount isn't in the source, it says so instead of guessing. When a post has no recipe in it, Tarifly asks for a screenshot or the video — it never makes one up.

Save the recipes you love

  • TikTok links: share the post to Tarifly; it reads the caption and cover image.
  • Screenshots: up to six at a time, read in the order you choose.
  • Videos saved to Photos: speech and on-screen text are read on your iPhone. Tarifly doesn't download videos from links.
  • Pasted text, or a recipe you write yourself.
  • Instagram: take a screenshot of the recipe or paste the caption.

Your recipes, beautifully organized

Clear ingredient lists and numbered steps, ingredient groups like “For the sauce” kept as the source wrote them, search and favorites, and nutrition estimates where the ingredients allow it. A recipe with missing steps or ingredients can be completed later from a screenshot, video or text.

Cook with confidence

Cook mode shows one large step at a time, offers timers when a step names a time (pause or add a minute), and keeps the screen on. The shopping list adds matching ingredients together and takes anything you add by hand. Cook mode and the shopping list are free for everyone.

Tarifly Plus

Unlimited supported recipe imports, a weekly meal plan for breakfast, lunch and dinner, serving scale up to 8× carried to your shopping list, and a shopping list grouped by aisle. Some import methods require a compatible Apple Intelligence device: recipes written as paragraphs are read with on-device Apple Intelligence. On other iPhones, recipes written as lists still import. Cloud processing is not currently available.

No account. No ads. No tracking.

There is no sign-up, no email, no advertising identifier, and no cross-app tracking. Your recipes are kept on your iPhone and are not sent to us — see the privacy policy for exactly what does and does not leave your device.

What you get for free

  • 5 imports per week (a rolling 7-day window), counted when you save an imported recipe. Recipes you write yourself don't count.
  • Unlimited saved recipes, with a review screen before anything is saved.
  • Calorie and macro estimates.
  • Cook mode and the shopping list.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

At Tarifly, your privacy is a product feature—not a marketing line. Your recipes belong to you.

1. Data collection & processing

  • No account required. Tarifly does not ask you to create an account or provide a name, phone number, or social login to use the app.
  • On your iPhone. Recipes are read on your iPhone: on-device Apple Intelligence where available, and on-device text recognition for screenshots and video frames. Your recipes, meal plan, shopping list and preferences are stored on your iPhone (SwiftData) and are not sent to us or synced to any Tarifly server. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, that backup includes them; backups are handled by Apple under your Apple Account.
  • TikTok links. When you share a TikTok link, Tarifly asks TikTok for that post’s caption and cover image. As with any web request, TikTok receives the link and your network address. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • No cloud processing. In this version Tarifly does not send recipe content, captions, screenshots, video frames or transcripts to any server for processing. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first and the app will say so before anything is sent.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal data.
  • Share Extension. When you share a link, screenshot or video into Tarifly, it is processed on your iPhone only to extract a recipe for you. Shared media is deleted once the import is saved or cancelled; a failed import keeps it so you can retry, until you discard it. A screenshot may be kept as the recipe’s cover image.

2. Subscriptions & payments

Tarifly Plus subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes payment. We do not receive or store your full card number.

3. Analytics

Tarifly keeps a small set of counters on your device so we can tell whether the app is actually useful — for example how many recipes were imported, whether a cook session was finished, and whether a shopping list was ticked off. These counters are stored in a separate on-device database, are kept at day-level resolution, and are deleted after 90 days.

They never leave your iPhone. There is no analytics SDK, no third-party tracker, and no upload: Tarifly has no server that receives them and no account to attach them to. No advertising identifier, device identifier, or user identifier is generated or stored, and no recipe title, link, creator name, or caption is ever recorded — only fixed event labels. Deleting the app deletes them.

We use no crash-reporting SDK. Aggregate crash and download statistics provided by Apple in App Store Connect are anonymised by Apple before we see them; we never receive per-user data.
